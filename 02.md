# Section II: Members of the Association

## Article IV: Membership
1. POLANA has active and passive members. 
2. Active members must be Bachelor, Master or doctoral students of the University of Zurich, ETH Zurich, ZHAW, ZHdK or PHZH. They can vote at the GA and can be elected to the Board and appointed as auditors.
3. Passive members consist of alumni, staff, faculty, students of other universities and other natural persons who are supporters of POLANA's goals. They may attend the GA and make proposals, but have no right to vote and cannot be elected for the Board or appointed as auditor. 
4. An active member who is no longer a student becomes a passive member.

## Article V: Membership Entry
1. POLANA membership is defined by the members list. Membership begins when a person completes POLANA's registration form, agreeing to these statutes and to the collection of personal data, which consists exclusively of their name, contact information and student status (university and level). The Board may refuse a registration within 30 days under Article VII. Members are required to update their student status when it changes.

## Article VI: Membership Rights and Obligations
1. The list of POLANA members is regularly updated by the Board.
2. Only POLANA members may attend the GA, and only active members may vote and be elected to the Board.
3. Each active member of POLANA gets one vote at the GA.
4. Members and Board representatives are required to abide by the purposes of the organisation and the Statutes.
5. Any member can terminate their membership through the following means:
    &emsp;Written or oral notification to the Board.  
    &emsp;Death.  
6. A resignation takes effect when the Board receives it, unless the member names a later date. The Board confirms it and updates the members list.
7. A member who has resigned has no right to POLANA assets and cannot claim refunds of any donations or contributions they made.

## Article VII: Membership Exclusion
1. The Board reserves the right to refuse/terminate the membership of any person in case of misconduct or unconstitutional practice.
2. The excluded member may appeal the Board’s decision to the next GA, which decides finally.
3. The excluded member has no right to any reimbursement of any donations, membership fees or contributions that they have paid. Likewise, they cannot claim any account, require any inventory, or demand the liquidation of POLANA.

## Article VIII: Membership Fee
1. For every semester, the members of POLANA pay a membership fee, agreed upon by the ordinary GA every semester.

## Article IX: The Board
1. The Board serves as the executive body of POLANA. It manages the association within the framework of the statutes and the directives of the GA. In particular, the Board:  
  &emsp;a. Decides on the direction and goals of the association within the framework set by the statutes and the GA.  
  &emsp;b. Decides on the projects to be developed and maintains contact with partners.  
  &emsp;c. Admits new members, while retaining a right of refusal.  
  &emsp;d. Records resignations of members.  
  &emsp;e. Convenes the GA and draws up its agenda.  
  &emsp;f. Keeps a list of members of the association up to date.  
  &emsp;g. Organises and manages events and fundraisers, and communicates them to members.  

## Article X: Board Membership Election
1. The Board consists of a minimum of three members, which must include a President, Main Event Organiser, and a Treasurer, which must be distinct people, and a Vice President, who may simultaneously have another role, except that of President. 
2. The Board is elected at each ordinary GA (except the Treasurer, see Article X §3); each member’s term runs until the next election at the next ordinary GA.
3. The Treasurer is elected at the spring ordinary GA; their term runs until the next election at the next spring ordinary GA of the following year. 
4. The Board elects the Vice President from among its members for a term of one semester at the first meeting of the new Board.
5. Members of the Board are eligible for re-election.
6. All members of the Board are separately elected and voted on.

## Article XI: Board Membership Termination
1. Every Board member may resign through a written or electronic announcement to the Board. If this happens, a replacement functionary may be voted on at a Board meeting.
2. If a member of the Board cannot carry out their full term for reasons other than resignation a replacement functionary may be voted on at a Board meeting.
3. After being voted for by the Board, a newly chosen replacement for the President or Treasurer must also be approved at a GA, which should be arranged promptly.
4. If a Board member is absent for three consecutive conventions of the Board without warning the President or Vice President, and without a valid reason for absence, the Board may declare the seat vacant; Article XI §2 applies.

## Article XII: Board Membership Roles
1. The tasks of the primary functionaries of the Board include, but are not limited to, the following:  
  &emsp;a. The President presides over the association and organises the GA. They are responsible for the organisational management and initiation of the organisation of events. They act as the legal representative for all documents that mention the name “POLANA” in reference to the association.  
  &emsp;b. The Vice President is the deputy president. They are responsible for the administrative tasks of the association, such as keeping the list of members.  
  &emsp;c. The Main Event Organiser oversees the organisation, coordination and scheduling of events.  
  &emsp;d. The Treasurer is responsible for the accounting of the association. They are responsible for the financial report of every organized activity, as well as regular internal financial updates and a financial report at each ordinary GA. The Treasurer is responsible for carrying out outgoing payments and for receiving outstanding payments, fees, and subsidies.  
  &emsp;&emsp;i. All documents pertaining to finances must be signed by both the President and Treasurer (as a secondary legal body).  
2. The Board may include additional functionaries, including, but not limited to positions as:
  &emsp;a. Communications Manager,  
  &emsp;b. ETH Responsible,  
  &emsp;c. UZH Responsible,  
  &emsp;d. External Relations,  
  &emsp;e. Event Organiser,   
  &emsp;f. IT Manager.  
3. Upon the decision of the Board, additional functionaries may be created or removed.


## Article XIII: Board Operations
1. The Board meets freely, without a fixed schedule.
2. The Board can only convene and decide legally if at least half of the Board members are present.
3. Decisions of the Board are taken by a majority vote of the present members, each having one vote.
4. In the event of a tie, members of the following roles have tiebreaking privileges:
  &emsp;a. President,  
  &emsp;b. Vice President,  
  &emsp;c. Treasurer,  
  &emsp;d. Main Event Organiser.  
Should the tiebreaker be absent, unable, or unwilling to cast their vote, the next member in line breaks the tie. Should the tie remain unbroken after this process, then no decision is reached, and the matter is postponed until the next meeting.
5. The Board reports on its activities to the GA.
