# Section VI: Statutes

## Article XXIII: Duties under the Statutes
1. Every Board member of the association is expected to have read the statutes, know them, and accept them. Every member is expected to be familiar with the goal and purpose of the association found within the statutes as well as their rights and obligations to POLANA.

## Article XXIV: Adjustments to the Statutes
1. The statutes may only be amended by a resolution of the General Assembly, taken by a two-thirds majority under Article XVI. The Board or any member may propose amendments under Article XV. The full text of each proposed amendment must be published with the agenda.
2. Amendments enter into force on the day the GA adopts them, unless the GA sets a later date. The amended text replaces the previous version, and Article XXIX records the date of adoption.
