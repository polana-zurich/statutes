# Section V: Task Forces

## Article XX: Role
1. The Board may set up task forces to carry out specific tasks.
2. The Board may delegate part of its competencies to a group of POLANA members, without this transfer having any effect on the general policy of the association or the general competencies of the Board.
3. A task force may be created for a limited period (provisional task force) for a single event, or they can be created indefinitely until dismissal.

## Article XXI: Creation
1. The creation and dissolution of the task force is the exclusive competence of the Board.
2. Any member of POLANA can become a member of a task force.
3. Only in the case of external collaborations can an external person become a member of a task force.
4. The Board may change the members of the task force freely.
5. The Board may dissolve the task force at any time.

## Article XXII: Functioning
1. Task forces work independently from the Board and must regularly report on their activities, which must be approved by the Board, with the Board being able to request additional information on their activity at any point in time.
2. Only the Board can fix the budget of the task force. 
3. An existing task force must submit a report to the Board at the end of its activity or at least once a year.
4. A task force must submit a complete account at least once a year so that the accounts can be presented to the GA.
