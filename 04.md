# Section IV: Accounting and Auditing

## Article XVII: Accounting
1. The corporate and accounting year begins on the 1st of January.
2. The Board presents the semesterly financial report of the preceding semester at each ordinary GA, as well as a financial prospect for the following semester.
3. The yearly financial report is kept by the Treasurer.

## Article XVIII: Auditing (Control Body)
1. An auditor is responsible for auditing the accounts and the financial report presented by the Board.
2. The auditor is entitled to:  
  &emsp;a. Require at any time the account statements and accounting documents of the association, as well as check the status of the cash register at any point in time,  
  &emsp;b. Convene a GA in the event of serious malfunctions in the association.  

## Article XIX: Auditor Elections
1. The auditor is to be appointed by the Board following the Spring ordinary GA, or after the resignation of the former auditor. 
2. The auditor’s term lasts one year.
3. Any active non-Board member of the association may be appointed as an auditor.
4. The auditor may resign through a written or electronic announcement to the Board. If this happens, a replacement auditor may be voted on at a Board meeting.
5. If the auditor cannot carry out their full term for reasons other than resignation, a replacement auditor may be voted on at a Board meeting.
