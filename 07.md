# Section VII: Liabilities and Signature

## Article XXV: Liability
1. The commitments and liabilities of POLANA are only guaranteed by the association’s assets.
2. Members do not assume any personal liability or obligation to make additional payments for the commitments made by the association.

## Article XXVI: Signature
1. The President and the Treasurer have joint power over all financial operations in line with any decisions of the GA.
2. For any commitment other than financial, particularly pertaining to legal commitments, the President and Vice President have joint legal power: pertaining to the signing of documents.
3. The legal power of the Treasurer and President may be delegated to another Board member by the Treasurer and the President respectively, in their areas of competence; no person signs in two capacities.
