# Section III: The General Assembly

## Article XIV: Role
1. The General Assembly (GA), consisting of active and passive members, is the supreme governing body of the association, and determines and controls its activities. It makes all decisions that are not the responsibility of the Board, with its functions consisting of, but not limited to:  
  &emsp;a. Electing the Board,  
  &emsp;b. Reviewing the finances,  
  &emsp;c. Approving any amendments to the statutes,  
  &emsp;d. Proposing and discussing events, ideas, goals and directions.  

## Article XV: Operation
1. The GA meets at least twice a year, at the beginning of the autumn and the spring semester (the ordinary GAs). 
2. Further, extraordinary GAs may be convened at any time under Article XV §4 and Article XVIII §2b. 
3. Every GA has the same powers and its decisions are equally binding; it takes place regardless of the number of members present. The regular Board elections take place at the ordinary GAs.
4. The GA may be convened at the request of at least one fifth of the active members or by the decision of the Board.
5. The Board publishes the agenda at least one week before each GA.
6. Members may submit proposals in writing at any time; those received 10 days before a GA are added to the agenda, which is sent to all members 7 days before it. Free motions may be raised before or during a GA and decided without being on the agenda, except on statute amendments, dissolution, exclusions, and Board elections or dismissals. The Board may reject a proposal or free motion only if it is unlawful, contrary to these statutes, or outside the GA’s competence, giving reasons; the proposer may appeal to the GA.
7. The Board may hold any GA in person, online or in hybrid form, provided every participating member can follow the debate, speak and vote. The invitation states the format and how to join.
8. During the GA, minimally, minutes of election results and decisions taken must be kept.
9. The minute-taker must be voted for at the beginning of the GA according to the rules in Article XVI §4.

## Article XVI: Voting
1. At the beginning of the GA, a vote counter(s) is(are) appointed and fixed in the protocol.
2. Each active member has the right to vote at the GA and has one vote, except:  
  &emsp;a. Members who joined POLANA after the invitation to a GA was sent may attend that GA but not vote.  
3. The Board is responsible for checking the right to vote of those present before the GA starts.
4. The GA decides by a simple majority of the votes cast; abstentions and blank or invalid votes are not counted. Changes to the statutes require two-thirds of the votes cast. During voting, a candidate or option needs a majority of the votes cast; if none reaches it, a second round is held between the candidates or options with the most votes. In the case of a tie in the second round, members of the following roles have tiebreaking privileges:  
  &emsp;a. President,  
  &emsp;b. Vice President,  
  &emsp;c. Treasurer,  
  &emsp;d. Main Event Organiser.  
Should the tiebreaker be absent, unable, or unwilling to cast their vote, the next member in line breaks the tie. A member is unable to tiebreak the election of any Board position if they candidate themselves. Should the tie remain unbroken after this process, the election is decided by drawing lots, conducted, in a fair manner, by the minute-taker.
5. Voting is done non-anonymously unless six or more members request an anonymous vote.
